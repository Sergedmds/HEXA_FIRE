Methodological Note & Sensor Calibration

Satellite-observed detections represent radiometer-flagged thermal anomalies (375m VIIRS / 1km MODIS) during clear-sky overpasses. Cloud cover attenuation, differing orbital overpass times, and dense vegetation canopy shielding must be considered when evaluating inter-regional differences.
